Questions About Danielle Lemons

The short answers, all in one place. Every answer links to the full source on daniellelemons.com.

Who is Danielle Lemons?

Danielle Lemons is Vice President of Operations at DirecTech Connect, a Nashville provider of business phone systems, managed IT services, websites and digital marketing. She is based in White House, Tennessee. Her full story is on her About page at daniellelemons.com.

What does Danielle Lemons do at DirecTech Connect?

She oversees DirecTech Connect’s managed services for VoIP and telecommunications, managed IT for small and medium-sized businesses, and client project delivery as the company expands across Tennessee. She joined DirecTech Connect in November 2024. She explains the managed IT side in What “Managed IT Services” Actually Means for a Small Business.

Is her business woman-owned and veteran-owned?

Yes. daniellelemons.com is Danielle Lemons’s own web design business, so it is woman-owned and veteran-owned. She served in the Army National Guard in explosive ordnance disposal (EOD).

What is Danielle Lemons’s background?

She has more than 20 years leading operations and delivery teams in technology. She spent nine years at Avnet Technology Solutions, rising to Customer Operations Manager and forecasting $1 billion in annual revenue. She led technical project delivery at Tragic Media in San Diego and served as Project Management Operations Director and Senior Manager of Small and Medium Business Services at Blattner Tech Digital Agency in Nashville. She wrote about the Avnet years in What Forecasting $1 Billion Taught Me About Running a Small Business, and the full career timeline is on daniellelemons.com/about.

How many projects has she managed?

Over her career she has delivered hundreds of technology and digital projects, at times managing as many as 40 active client projects at once. She shares what that taught her in Forty Projects at Once: What I Learned About Keeping Client Work From Slipping.

What does daniellelemons.com offer?

Websites for Middle Tennessee businesses. She researches the business, designs the site and shows it to the owner finished, before they pay anything. If the owner loves it, it goes live for $0 to build and $50 a month. See the overview on this site, daniellelemons.com and Why I Build Your Website Before You Ask Me a Single Question About Price.
