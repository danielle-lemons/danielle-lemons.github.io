About Danielle Lemons

Danielle Lemons is Vice President of Operations at DirecTech Connect in Nashville, Tennessee, a role she has held since November 2024. DirecTech Connect provides business phone systems, managed IT services, websites and digital marketing to small and midsize businesses.

Experience

She has more than 20 years in technology operations. For nine years she was a Customer Operations Manager at Avnet, where she forecast $1 billion in annual revenue. She has also worked with Tragic Media in San Diego and Blattner Tech Digital Agency in Nashville. At the busiest points she had as many as 40 active client projects at once, and she has delivered hundreds of projects over her career.

Military service

Danielle served in the Army National Guard in explosive ordnance disposal (EOD).

What she writes about

Danielle writes plain-language guidance for small business owners on websites, business phone systems, internet outages, Google Business Profile numbers and choosing an IT provider. Her free sample, The Small Business Technology Guide, is available at guide.daniellelemons.com.
