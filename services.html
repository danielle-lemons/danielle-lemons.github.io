Websites by Danielle Lemons

Websites for Middle Tennessee businesses, built before you pay a thing.

Through daniellelemons.com, Danielle Lemons researches your business, designs the site and shows it to you finished, before you pay anything. If you love it, it goes live for $0 to build and $50 a month for everything.

Pricing

  • $0 to build it. Research, design, copy and photos. You see the finished site before you pay anything.
  • $50 a month. One flat rate for hosting, security, backups, lead alerts and your editing panel. No setup fee, and you can cancel anytime. The $50 rate is locked in for the life of your site.
  • 10% off when you pay the year. Twelve months up front is $540 instead of $600.

Bigger jobs, like a full redesign, extra pages, online ordering or booking, are quoted before any work starts. Refer someone who needs a site and take $50 off your bill when they sign up.

What is included

  • Hosting
  • SSL certificate
  • Daily backups
  • Security and updates
  • Lead alerts to your phone and email
  • Your own editing panel for words, photos, prices and hours, live in a minute

How it works

  1. Tell her about your business. Five minutes on the phone or the form at daniellelemons.com: your name, what you do, where you are and where you want to be found.
  2. She builds it. You look at it. She researches your business, your competition and your customers, then designs and writes the site. You get a link to the finished site. No mockups, no deposit.
  3. Love it? It goes live. Your domain, your own editing panel and lead alerts on your phone. If you do not love it, you owe nothing.

Examples of sites she has built, from an electrical contractor in Nashville to a church in Madison and BBQ catering in Gallatin, are on daniellelemons.com/work.
