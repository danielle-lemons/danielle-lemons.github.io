Clicked a Suspicious Link? What to Do Next, by Danielle Lemons

DirecTech Connect is a telecom and IT provider for small businesses in Nashville, covering managed IT, cybersecurity, phones and websites. Clicking a bad link happens to careful people. What matters is what you do in the next few minutes.

Right after you click

  1. Stop. Don't type anything into the page that opened, especially a password, a code or a card number. Close the tab.
  2. Don't open downloads. If a file downloaded, leave it alone. Disconnect the device from Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable if anything installed or the computer starts acting strangely.
  3. Change the password from another device. If you entered a password, change it right away from a different, trusted device, starting with that account and any other account that uses the same password. Then turn on multi-factor authentication.
  4. Call your bank the right way. If you entered card or bank details, call the number on the back of your card, not a number from the page or message.
  5. Tell IT at work immediately. A two-minute report is far better than hiding it. The person who clicks is rarely the problem; the person who stays quiet gives the attacker time.
  6. Scan and watch. Run a full scan with your security software and watch your accounts and email for login alerts over the next few weeks.

Warning signs of a suspicious link

  • Urgency or fear: "your account will be closed today," "payment failed," "unusual sign-in."
  • A sender name that looks right but an address that doesn't match the company.
  • Link text that doesn't match the real address. Hover or long-press to check before you tap.
  • A login page you reached from a message instead of going to the site yourself.
  • Requests for a password, verification code, gift cards or card numbers. No legitimate company needs those through a link.
  • Shortened links or misspelled domains, like an extra letter or a different ending.

The one habit that prevents most of this

Never log in from a link. Open the app or type the address yourself.
